Cold-Email-Outreach

Terms of Use

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Cold-Email-Outreach is a private software utility created and maintained by Jackson Ding for personal engineering networking and contact management. This public page describes the tool; it is not a public service or an invitation to create an account.

Intended use

The utility maintains a private professional contact tracker and prepares unsent email drafts for the authorized owner's review. The user is responsible for reviewing each message, verifying factual statements, respecting applicable outreach rules, and manually choosing whether to send it. This software does not automatically send email.

Google authorization

Google account access requires voluntary OAuth authorization. The owner may revoke access at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation may disable the tool's Gmail and Sheets features. See the Privacy Policy for details about information handling.

Availability

The application is supplied for personal use and may be changed, suspended, or discontinued without notice. No guarantee is made about the availability of third-party platforms or the accuracy of externally supplied professional contact data.
